Privacy policy

Calendar Sync is a personal tool used only by its owner. It reads the owner's own sleep and health metrics (Google Health API, read-only) to plan sleep and schedule blocks on the owner's own Google Calendar.

  • Data is read only for the owner's account and only to plan their calendar.
  • Data is stored only in the owner's own Google Apps Script project and Google Calendar.
  • No data is sold, shared, or sent to anyone else.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
